Chat with Work Local Agent lets Chat with Work search and read the folders you choose on this Mac, and nothing else.

  • Four read-only tools: roots, search, list and read. Nothing is written, deleted or run.
  • Only the folders you share, and never the secrets inside them.
  • Outbound only: no port is opened on your Mac.

This installs the cww command in /usr/local/bin and a per-user background agent that stays idle until you pair this Mac.
